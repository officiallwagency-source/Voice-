Download ke baare mein: Browsers seedhe TTS ki audio file nahi dete (security ki wajah se). Isliye "Download Audio" tab ka audio record karta hai jab tak text bola ja raha hai. Best Chrome/Edge desktop par kaam karta hai; phone browsers par yeh feature support nahi karte.
